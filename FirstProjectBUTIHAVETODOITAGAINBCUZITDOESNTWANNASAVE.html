Who is Alt?

ALT is a music project created by Nightcove_theFox in 2025 that is depicted by a human version of a yellow rabbit. He makes dark electronic and rock alternative music, and he makes his own videos and songs and lyrics.

The History Behind ALT

ALT was created originally in 2021 by Nightcove_theFox as a 'alt'er ego that came into existence when Nightcove_theFox found becoming Glitchtrap for videos harder. The two hardly got along, and ALT soon craved freedom. Nightcove wanted the views and the love from fans that ALT got, as he got a great amount more than Nightcove, so she forced him to keep making videos. He protested through songs, making More, released in 2021, to show exactly what Nightcove wanted and how the fans weren't exactly helping. Heard It All Before, released in June 2022, was about the same thing, albeit more directly. A Bad Thing, released in February 2023, highlighted the relationship between ALT and Nightcove.

After that, ALT took to more direct measures, making screens in his videos glitch, teasing a new song. This one was 8.5.12.16.13.5, released July 31st, 2024, and it showed directly what ALT wanted, freedom from the cosplay videos, freedom from Nightcove. On August 7th, 2024, Nightcove posted a video titled 'An Adventure With Glitchtrap'. This was an interactive video filled with lore about ALT, and if the viewer followed the game well, they could free ALT.

ALT returned in February to Nightcove's channel, hoping to claim revenge. He released Tragic Ending on February 15, 2025, and from there held Nightcove captive and soon found out Nightcove had attempted to bring ALT back to make more videos. He went to Nightcove, angry, and found she was gone. He made multiple shorts and videos attempting to rally fans and viewers to find Nightcove, and on April 12th, 2025, released a music video titled The Villain. A week after, Nightcove posted a video titled 'This is Not Goodbye', and it was ALT's separation from Nightcove's channel. He had one final message to say to the fans, the dearest villains.

ALT went on to post covers of songs until he was ready to post his first single on his own channel, MOLOTOV.
